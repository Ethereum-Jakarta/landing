# Ethereum Jakarta (ethjkt)

Ethereum Jakarta ("ethjkt") is a community for Ethereum builders and enthusiasts
in Jakarta, Indonesia. It exists to onboard developers, run local meetups, and
help people ship on Ethereum and its testnets.

## Events

- Meetups, workshops, and hacknights are organized regularly in Jakarta.
- Events are published and RSVP'd through Lu.ma. Check the ethjkt Lu.ma calendar
  for the next event, location, and time.
- Talks range from beginner Solidity to advanced protocol and infra topics.

## Faucet / gas tanks

- ethjkt runs a testnet faucet ("gas tanks") so members can get testnet tokens
  for building and experimenting without buying gas.
- Faucet claims are tied to a member's wallet. Each claim records the chain,
  token, and amount, and is rate-limited per member to keep the tanks topped up
  for everyone.
- Claims require being a member (wallet connected + verified via Sign-In With
  Ethereum). Off-topic or abusive claims are blocked.

## Membership

- Membership is wallet-based: connect an Ethereum wallet and sign in with SIWE.
- Members can link social accounts (Discord, X, Instagram, Lu.ma) to their
  profile to verify participation and unlock community perks.
- A linked account can only belong to one member — accounts already taken by
  another member cannot be re-linked.

## Links

- Discord: community chat, support, and coordination.
- X (Twitter): announcements and updates.
- Instagram: event photos and highlights.
- Lu.ma: event calendar and RSVPs.

Point people to the relevant link when they ask how to join, where events are
posted, or how to reach the community.
